Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

Running Personal OS is an early-stage running training and athlete-data application developed by an independent developer. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when a user chooses to connect fitness services such as Garmin.

Information processed

Depending on the features a user enables, the application may process account identifiers, training plans, structured workouts, activity and fitness information, and related athlete-provided context needed to provide the requested training experience. The application is designed to request and process only information needed for enabled functionality.

Garmin data

When Garmin integration is enabled, Garmin-derived data is used only to provide user-requested functionality such as training-plan integration, workout delivery, training analysis, synchronization, and related athlete-facing features.

Garmin-derived data is not sold, rented, or licensed to third parties.

User control and workout publishing

Training recommendations may originate from multiple sources, including user input, imported plans, analytical software, or AI-assisted tools. A recommendation does not by itself authorize publication to a Garmin account or device.

The application is designed so that device-directed training output requires an applicable user-approved training plan and authorization before publishing.

Service providers and AI

The application may use third-party infrastructure and software providers for hosting, storage, analytics, application operation, and AI-assisted analysis. Such providers may process information only as needed to provide application functionality.

Where AI-assisted features are used, they support analysis or recommendations; they do not replace the application's user-approval controls for device publishing.

Data security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used to protect information, including access controls, credential separation, and limiting access to information needed for application operation. No internet-connected system can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and deletion

Information is retained only as reasonably necessary to provide enabled functionality, maintain system integrity, satisfy applicable requirements, and support user-requested history. Users may request deletion of their application data and disconnection of connected services.

Sharing

Personal information and Garmin-derived data are not sold. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate requested features, or when required by law.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the application develops. Material changes will be reflected by updating this page and its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the project owner through the public GitHub profile associated with this project.
